decode_gate_flows: report per-body entropy and print whatever is readable

Terminating TLS is not the same as a readable body, and this script refuses to
assume either way. Every captured response is hex-decoded and reported with its
length, Shannon entropy and printable ratio, then printed only when those two
numbers say there is text to read. Bodies that are genuinely ciphertext are
reported as CIPHERTEXT rather than being mangled into the output, because
"unreadable" is itself a result worth having rather than noise to hide.

Thresholds are 7.0 bits/byte and 60 percent printable. The login body measured
7.67 and 7.99, well clear of text, while configuration responses such as server
environment and relay quality are expected to come back readable -- which is
the reason to run this over every captured response rather than only the login.
